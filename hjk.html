Jabbaar Pro

Desinger, Editor, Developer

Waxaan ahay Jabbaar Pro, waxaan bixiyaa adeegyo tayo sare leh oo ay ka mid yihiin Graphic Design, Video Editing, Motion Graphics, iyo Web Development.

Haddii aad rabto naqshado casri ah, muuqaallo soo jiidasho leh, ama website xirfad leh, ila soo xiriir maanta.

Our Services

Graphic Design
Waxaan sameeyaa logos, posters, flyers, banners, iyo naqshado casri ah oo soo jiidasho leh. Naqshad kasta waxaan diiradda saaraa tayada, hal-abuurka, iyo muuqaalka ganacsigaaga.
Video Editing
Waxaan tafatiraa muuqaalada si xirfad leh, anigoo ku daraya transitions, effects, subtitles, iyo motion graphics. Ujeedadaydu waa in muuqaalkaagu noqdo mid soo jiita daawadayaasha.
Web Development
Waxaan dhisaa websites casri ah, degdeg ah, kana shaqeeya mobile-ka iyo computer-ka. Website kasta waxaan u naqshadeeyaa si uu u noqdo mid qurux badan, fudud in la isticmaalo, isla markaana kor u qaada ganacsigaaga.
